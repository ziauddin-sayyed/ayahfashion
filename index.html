About Ayah Fashion

Started operation in 2003 by Founder Aqueel Yusuf Khan, Ayah Fashion established itself as a leading Manufacturer, Supplier & Wholesaler of Kids Wear and Ladies Wear in Mumbai. Our wide range primarily comprises kids Co Ord sets, embroidery tops, frocks, jumpsuits, night suits, kurti palazzo sets, girl one-piece dresses & ladies kurti sets — sourced from reliable vendors and re-checked for quality assurance.
